## 🎙️ Recording Tips for Top 1% Demo
1. **Resolution:** Record in full 1080p or 1440p (16:9 ratio).
2. **Audio:** Use a clean USB microphone or headset in a quiet room.
3. **Cursor Movement:** Move cursor smoothly and deliberately; avoid erratic or rapid twitching.
4. **Browser Setup:** Use Fullscreen mode (`F11`) with bookmarks bar hidden for a clean, app-like showcase.
