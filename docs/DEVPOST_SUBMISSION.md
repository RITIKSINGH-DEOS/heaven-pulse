### 📌 Project Title
**HeavenPulse**

### 📌 Elevator Pitch / Tagline
*An anonymous, zero-knowledge sanctuary combining clinical CBT cognitive deconstruction with live peer resonance to dismantle personal insecurity.*

---

### 💡 Inspiration
The US Surgeon General and the World Health Organization have declared youth mental health and emotional isolation the defining public health crisis of our generation. As students, we live this reality every day. When we experience acute self-doubt at 2:00 AM—paralyzing impostor syndrome before exams, severe body dysmorphia triggered by social media feeds, or social anxiety in group settings—we are convinced of a devastating lie: *"Everyone else is thriving; I am the only broken person in the room."*

Existing digital solutions fail us completely. Commercial AI chatbots feel sterile and mechanical, leaving users isolated with an unfeeling algorithm. Anonymous forums like Reddit or Discord expose vulnerable people to trolling, unsolicited direct messages, or painful silence. Meanwhile, professional therapy costs upwards of \$150/hour with 6-week campus counseling backlogs. 

We were inspired to build **HeavenPulse**: a 100% free, non-profit digital public sanctuary that bridges the gap between cold robotic chatbots and chaotic public forums.

---

### ⚙️ What It Does
HeavenPulse is a zero-knowledge emotional first-aid network that operates on a two-tiered healing model:

1. **The Sanctuary Intake (Zero-Login):** Users enter without an account, email, or digital footprint. They select their domain of vulnerability (*Impostor & Career, Body Image, Social Inadequacy, or Belonging*) and express their authentic inner critic. A deterministic safety air-gap continuously monitors for acute crisis, providing immediate 24/7 access to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
2. **The Live Peer Resonance Hub:** Instead of isolation, the system shows live community telemetry (*"420+ members processed this exact domain this week"*). Users see verified peer cards with glowing 🟢 **Online Now** pulse indicators, resonance counts, and distilled wisdom.
3. **The 5-Minute Ephemeral Empathy Bridge:** Users can connect with an online peer for an encrypted, time-bounded 5-minute chat. Anonymous nature-inspired aliases protect identities, while an automated PII firewall redacts phone numbers and social handles in real-time.
4. **The Clarity Mirror & The Release Ritual:** For both peer-connected users and solo introspective users, HeavenPulse applies Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) to classify the cognitive distortion (*Mind-Reading, Catastrophizing, Spotlight Trap, or All-or-Nothing thinking*). It contrasts the *Inner Critic's Illusion* against an evidence-based *Objective Reality Anchor*, guides an interactive 4-4-6 grounding breath pacer, and enables the user to permanently dissolve the burden from local memory through an interactive Release & Burn ritual.

### 🛠️ How We Built It
HeavenPulse is engineered with full-stack senior developer standards, strict type safety, and zero commercial bloat:

- **Frontend Architecture:** Built with **Next.js 15 (App Router)** and **TypeScript**, styled using a bespoke dark luxury design system in **Tailwind CSS**. It incorporates a custom HTML5 Canvas 3D particle sine-wave mesh with ambient floating embers and top-center spotlight illumination.
- **Cognitive Engine:** An evidence-based CBT classification engine that deterministically maps linguistic patterns to psychological fallacies and synthesizes Socratic reflection prompts.
- **Privacy & Security Air-Gap:** Client-side ephemeral session management with zero SQL/NoSQL database persistence. Personal Identifiable Information (PII) is intercepted via deterministic regex sanitizers before peer transit.
- **Automated Test Suite:** A standalone automated test suite (`tests/engine.test.ts` via `tsx`) verifying 100% passing tests across emergency crisis detection, PII redaction, CBT distortion classification, and peer resonance prioritization.

---

### 🧗 Challenges We Ran Into
- **Balancing Peer Empathy with Absolute Safety:** Open chat rooms are notoriously vulnerable to trolling, harassment, and trauma-dumping. We solved this by creating a 5-minute time-bounded chamber, disabling free-form profile creation, building an automated PII/handle filter, and providing curated empathy reaction drops.
- **Eliminating the "Toy Chatbot" Trap:** We did not want another generic ChatGPT wrapper. We engineered a hybrid deterministic psychological triage pipeline based on clinical thought records, ensuring every user receives actionable Socratic deconstruction rather than empty platitudes.
- **Zero-Trace Architecture:** Maintaining state across multi-screen flows without saving a single byte of user data to a permanent database required careful in-memory state coordination and automatic purge hooks upon tab closure.
